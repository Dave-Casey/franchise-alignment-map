Quadrant Filters

Perceptual
  • Qualifies: reusable models of the human listener: attention, salience, preference, fatigue, thresholds, intelligibility, or perceptual evaluation.
  • Disqualifies: DSP, rendering, acoustics, hardware, or product UX that only makes one product sound better.
Environmental
  • Qualifies: reusable physical-world representations: rooms, cabins, noise fields, wind, acoustic scenes, topology, sensors, or environment embeddings.
  • Disqualifies: environment context used only as an input to listener preference, or hardware design without reusable environment inference.
Orchestration
  • Qualifies: cross-device handoff, state sharing, compute placement, capability negotiation, latency/power coordination, SDKs, APIs, runtimes, or fabric primitives.
  • Disqualifies: mode switching or DSP control inside one device; algorithm deployment without reusable cross-target coordination.
Trust
  • Qualifies: privacy-preserving personalization, secure inference, model protection, identity/access, consent, auditability, compliance, or regulated deployment.
  • Disqualifies: general user confidence, comfort, safety, cybersecurity, encryption, privacy UI, or internal data governance without perceptual trust architecture.
